# Drums

## How it plays

16 pads, velocity from where you hit. A bead lane shows the selected pad's steps (tap a bead to toggle). Pass lines under the lane show each recorded pass. Settings: kit, per-pad tune/decay/level, swing, lane clearing.

## Questions

- Does recording a beat in passes make it easy to back out a bad pass?
- Does replace (`↻`) on drums do what you expect (only the pads you play)?
- Are the pads big enough to hit without looking?
