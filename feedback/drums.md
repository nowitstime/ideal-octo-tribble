# Drums

## How it plays

The beat is written, not played. One row per sound. Tap the beat squares (1 2 3 4) to pick which beats it lands on, and the small `1 e & a` buttons to pick where inside the beat (on the beat, the "e", the "&", the "a"). The dots in each square show the result. `vary` cycles off / light / wild: ghost notes, the odd dropped hit and a fill on the last beat, different every loop pass. Tap a row's colour square to hear it; `×` removes the row; `+ sound` adds one.

Examples: kick every other beat = beats 1 3. Backbeat snare = beats 2 4. Eighth hats = all beats with `1` and `&`. Offbeat open hat = all beats with only `&`.

Settings: kit, per-sound tune/decay/level, swing.
