# Map

## How it plays

The loop is a ring; the four instruments hang off it on strings and the progression threads in from above. Drag the tempo, pick 1/2/4 bars, quantize and click, play and record. Each square has a mute, each string a clear (tap ✕, tap again to confirm).

## Questions

- Can you tell what's recorded on each track at a glance?
- Is the clear on the strings easy to find, and hard to hit by accident?
- Do record options (`+`/`↻`, `1×`, count-in) read clearly in the transport?

## Notes

## Decided

- Pastel squares, strings and circle knobs; crisp, not sketchy.
