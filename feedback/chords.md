# Chords

## How it plays

One column per chord in the key. Slide up for brighter voicings, hold 7 / sus / add9 with another finger, drag across the strum strip. The note lane shows what's recorded (tap to delete); pass lines show each pass.

## Questions

- Is holding a chord for its full length natural, or would you rather tap?
- Does the progression marker help you play along?
